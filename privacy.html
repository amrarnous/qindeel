Privacy policy

Qindeel is built to be private by design. There is no account, no login and no backend that stores your worship.

What stays on your phone

Your azkar progress, streak, tasbeeh counts, bookmarks, reading position and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Location

If you allow it, Qindeel reads your device location (approximate or precise, as you choose in Android or iOS) to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. The calculation happens on your phone; your location is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can pick a city from the list instead.

Internet use

The Quran, azkar and prayer times work offline. When you open a tafsir, Qindeel downloads that text from the public jsDelivr CDN. Like any web request, the CDN sees your IP address, but no personal data, location or usage information is sent with it.

Notifications

Prayer and azkar reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. No push service or server is involved.

Advertising

Qindeel does not show ads and does not include any advertising SDK. It does not collect advertising identifiers or share your data with ad networks. If this ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change ships, and you will be asked for consent where the law requires it.

Launch list

If you join the launch list on this site, we keep your email only to send one message on launch day. We never sell or share it.
